About

Two halves of the same thing

I am in Grade 10 in the IB programme, studying Chinese, English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, and Economics. On paper that looks like a scattered list. It is not: each one is a different way of describing the same world, and what interests me most are the seams between them — where an economic model meets an ecosystem, or where a scientific finding has to be argued for in plain language.

The other half of my life is performance and practice: competitive debate and speech, essay writing, the guzheng, calligraphy, and dance. I have come to think these are not separate from the academic side at all. A debate round and a lab report ask for the same thing — say what you mean, and show your evidence.
